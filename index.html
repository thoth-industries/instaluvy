This is Instaluvy's "official" site - pages pulled from a github repo. We don't like the competitive glam world - and let's be honest, web pages with all their clicky-movey stuff and all the stock art and photography have pretty much fallen into the glam world.

This is probably looking to you a bit "plain Jane." Lots of reasons for that, but one is: we want you to enjoy the cheapo clicky-movey yum yum of our panderThings. Which, once we are moving - you should be able to get at various local places. Or ask your friends if they've got 'em.

panderThings are meant to live in a different economy from the glam economy. One that pre-dates the glam economy. Yeah, they're glammy cheapo clicky-movey things. But they're also a kind of invitation for a rethink about what kind of economy your soul lives in.
