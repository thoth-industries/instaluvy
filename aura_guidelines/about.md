##About aura guidelines

#who / when

We encourage you to use aura guidelines for some of your best or most important work.

Regarding other work: we encourage you to share only with friends, loved ones, and people who can help you with the direction or evaluation of your work.  We don't advise 
sharing on social media with everyone.  Why?  This could take books and books to explain.  We have read thousands and thousands of pages in the area of 
the philosophy, the sociology, the psychology of art.  And these are simply our suggestions.  We aren't the boss of you.  Go ahead and do what you want.

#why
Many, many, many reasons why.
"Fad prevention" is the easiest one to blurt out, but aura preservation goes so much deeper than this.
Walter Benjamin and Theodor Adorno really stand out here, but there is a host of important thinking around this matter, though often not using the 
word "aura."
