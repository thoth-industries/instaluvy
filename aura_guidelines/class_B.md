## Aura guidelines for Instaluvy Class B items

An Instaluvy Class B item is a so-called "shirt song."
Shirt songs are a dee-lish way of doing a bit of sweet virtue signalling while you do the more "real" social thing with music.
I.e., people share via getting the music off the QR code of your phone.  And are kindly requested to refrain from sharing the 
music, art, poetry in other ways.  I.e., they need to get the shirt themselves, or find someone who's got the shirt.

Genuine Shirt Songs are songs attached to some kind of important cause, including the cause of more highly developed or seriously horizon-expanding art.
You can make a cheap cowboy pop song about walking along the beach and falling in love, slap the QR code on a shirt, 
or a song that's an advertisement for a pool party next month, and call it a Crappy Shirt Song just fine.  
And many Crappy Shirt songs will be a lot better than Genuine Shirt Songs.

# dissemination
Please do not share.
This item comes with a shirt, some other clothing item, a theatre piece, or is that theatre piece itself.
The two belong together.
People who want the song need to find someone who has the shirt or other clothing item, and get the song from the qr code.
Or buy a thingthing that got the QR code on it.
A big exception here is people who really genuinely have no smartphones at all.
For such people: please let them have the location of the song / item.  With the same stipulations of 
this file.


# discourse
(e.g., "talk-around" protocol)
Feel free to say whatever you want about this when in live groups of 10 or less.
In peer-reviewed scholarly journals, also feel free to say whatever you want about it.  Ditto niche journals which are highly relevant to the song in some way.

In journals more populist than peer-reviewed scholarly journals, and in groups other than live groups of 10 or less, or one-on-one conversations not observable by the public, we highly recommend:

"Talk around" the song, the lyrics.  Select a feature which you find especially important, and talk about that.  Hit Google Scholar.  See what relevant scholars say.  Google for literature, art on the subject matter.  Talk about that.  This will likely have a lot of individual cultivation and social cultivation benefits.  Much more than blurting out your feelings about the song.

#reception
We don't have any reception guidelines for this class of items.



#liturgical fad prevention: including possible media and discourse sabbath
Please DO NOT distribute LINK or actual file on soical media of any kind.
Keep it social.  Share in vivo: in the supermarket, in the park.  When having tea.

sharing sabbath:
Please don't share or talk about this during the months of May and June or November and December, letting these things go on a kind of "sharing sabbath."
Please note: these files will likely be unavailable also DURING the sharing sabbath.  Yes, you bought the shirt.  But the sharing sabbath sticks.

#exceptions
Keep in mind those in your neighborhood who have mobility difficulties.  If they are up for visiting, please visit them.  If it seems 
right to share this with them, go ahead and share it.  See also the note under "discourse" on people without smart phones.
